Support

Looking to get real flowers from Thistle Dew Green? Our primary line for any request, big or small, is email at karen@thistledewflowers.com. Here's what to know before you reach out.

How can I request flowers?

The easiest way to reach us is by email at karen@thistledewflowers.com — that's our primary contact for any flower request. You can also send a direct message on Instagram (@thistledewgreen) or text us at 719-362-6518.

How far in advance should I reach out?

It depends on the size of your event. Custom wedding flowers usually take a few months of planning to source and grow the right blooms, while something simple like a single corsage can often be put together within a day. When in doubt, reach out early — we'll let you know what's realistic for your timeline.

What does it cost?

Thistle Dew Green runs on a needs-based system rather than a fixed price list. We keep things as affordable as possible — and often free — for proms, weddings, and other events for Colorado youth, while charging reasonable rates that let us keep the farm running. Tell us about your event and your situation, and we'll work out what makes sense together.

Do you deliver?

For large catered events like weddings, yes — delivery is available. For smaller orders like single bouquets, corsages, or potted plants, we currently don't offer delivery, so plan on picking up in Colorado Springs.

What flowers do you grow, and does that change with the seasons?

We grow what thrives in the Colorado climate, including tulips, ranunculus, snapdragons, zinnias, thistle, baby's breath, and more. Our growing season runs through spring, summer, and fall — winter weather puts a hard limit on what we can grow outdoors, so availability shifts throughout the year.
