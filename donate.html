Thistle Dew Green is a passion project by Karen Haddad. We price flowers on a needs-based system rather than a fixed rate, keeping them as affordable as possible — and often free — for proms, weddings, and other events for Colorado youth.

If you'd like to help us keep the farm running and keep flowers accessible for the community, a Venmo contribution goes a long way. Looking to request flowers for your own event instead? Visit our Support page for pricing and details.
