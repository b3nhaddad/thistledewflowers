Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Introduction

Thistle Dew Green ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website at thistledewflowers.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our site and what rights you have regarding your data.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information from visitors to this website. However, third-party services embedded on this site may collect data as described below.

Adcash & Advertising

We use Adcash to display advertisements on this site. Adcash and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the internet. This allows Adcash to show you more relevant ads.

For more information about how Adcash handles data, see Adcash's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies through Adcash and embedded third-party content (such as Instagram). These cookies may track your browsing activity across websites to serve relevant advertising and content. You can control and delete cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Third-Party Embedded Content

Pages on this site may include embedded content from Instagram and other third-party services. Embedded content from other websites behaves in the same way as if you had visited that website directly. These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, and embed additional tracking. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services.

Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external websites including Instagram, Venmo, and Local Flowers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to them.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be noted by updating the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
