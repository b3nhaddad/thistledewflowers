Organic Flowers, Grown with Love

Welcome to Our Farm

Thistle Dew Green is a small, non-profit organic flower farm run by Karen Haddad and her family. We grow specialty cut flowers with a deep commitment to nature, sustainability, and spreading the joy that flowers bring to every occasion — from weddings and proms to simple everyday bouquets, always with love.

What We Offer

🌸 Wedding Flowers

We create stunning, hand-picked arrangements that make your special day unforgettable. Every bloom grown fresh on our farm, with care.

🌹 Custom Bouquets

From simple everyday bunches to elaborate centerpieces, our seasonal blooms are available for any occasion. Ask us what's in season!

🌿 Organic & Sustainable

As a proud ASCFG member, we are dedicated to preserving the natural world while growing the finest specialty cut flowers.

Our Mission

Thistle Dew Green is a non-profit passion project. Every flower we grow and every arrangement we create comes from a place of love for nature and community. Donations help us continue providing flowers for weddings, proms, and events completely free of charge.
